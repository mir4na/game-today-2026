AFTER THE END

Death should have been your ending. Instead, an angel's mistake gives you one last chance—but your sins still outweigh your Blessings. To settle the balance, you must serve five days as an intern conductor aboard a train between the living world and the afterlife.

At the end of every line, lost souls are waiting for someone to lead them to the station where they belong. Inspect the living by day, uncover the dead by night, and complete your service before the train reaches its final destination.

After the end of a life comes one last journey. After the end of the line comes judgment. And when your final shift is over—where do you belong?

Your Daily Service

Day Shift — Inspect & Decide

Walk through the train, inspect identity cards and tickets, compare portraits and destinations, and decide who should leave at each station. Correct drop-offs earn Blessings. Careless decisions reduce your paycheck.

Night Service — Read & Deduce

When the train crosses into the Soul Line, the suspicious passengers reveal their true nature. Search their Soul Records for hidden statements, study the constellation route, and assign every soul to the station where they belong.

CONDUCTOR'S WARNING

Some passengers are already dead. Do not stamp their tickets during the day. Keep them aboard for Night Service.

Features

  • Two connected shifts: daytime document inspection and nighttime deduction aboard the same train.
  • Five days of escalating cases: more souls, harder anomalies, and increasingly complex constellation routes.
  • Evidence-driven decisions: inspect faces, IDs, tickets, destinations, shadows, and public records.
  • Interactive Soul Records: uncover hidden statements inside each passenger's biography.
  • Constellation puzzles: use routes, junctions, distance markers, and relational clues to place every soul.
  • A Blessings economy: meet your daily target and spend your earnings on Veil Notes, Radar Charges, and Swiftstep.
  • Route sign-off: finish early by tracing your signature and moving the train to its next stop.
  • Multiple outcomes: complete the internship and earn your place—or be sent somewhere far less forgiving.
SOUL LINE NOTICE

The answer is never written in one place. Read carefully, connect the clues, and remember: a familiar face can still belong to the dead.

Controls

  • A / D or Left / Right Arrow — Walk through the train
  • E — Interact
  • Tab — Open or close the Guidebook
  • Q — Switch between passenger documents
  • Esc — Close the current interface or pause
  • Mouse — Inspect, stamp, drag souls, trace signatures, and navigate menus

Will You Earn Your Place?

You have five days to prove that a life cut short can still amount to something. Keep the living on their proper route, lead the dead to their final station, and make every Blessing count.
